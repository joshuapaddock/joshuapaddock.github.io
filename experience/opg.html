01 Bench-test conditions for new motors

Skills Programming

The fuelling machines were getting new inverter-driven motors, and the bench tests had to cover the right inverter commands. I built a Python validation tool that turns raw machine logs into command-density fields for X, Y and rotational motion, drawn as Gaussian heat maps that show which commands needed to be tested.

The heat maps defined the bench-testing conditions for the new motors across 8 fuelling machines, and senior engineering signed off on the results.

02 Heavy-water drip-tray redesign

Skills Mechanical design Prototyping

I redesigned a heavy-water (D2O) drip tray that protects the Z-axis motors of the fuelling machine. Rapid 3D-printed prototypes let me work out an adaptive mounting configuration and test it in the field for fit and size. The design includes a custom gamma-resistant cable guard rated for a 40-year life.

The final design was validated for fit on 8 machines with zero impact on travel range.
