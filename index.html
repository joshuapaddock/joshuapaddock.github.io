About

I'm in my fifth year of Mathematics & Mechanical Engineering at Queen's University, graduating in May 2027. I like problems where the math and the machine meet: designing the hardware, wiring it, writing the controller, and then figuring out how to make it learn. In coursework, I'm most drawn to control theory (MTHE 430) and stochastic processes (MTHE 455).

Outside of engineering, I'm usually golfing, at the gym, or on a climbing wall. I also spend my downtime reading frontier AI lab papers and digging through balance sheets to pick stocks to beat the S&P 500.
